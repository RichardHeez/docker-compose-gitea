Questions

Before you buy.

Is it safe for electronics?

Yes. Unlike canned air it uses no propellant, so there is no cold spray and no residue. Start on speed 1 or 2 for keyboards and circuit boards, and hold fan blades still while you clean them.

How long does the battery last?

Run time depends on the speed you choose. Lower speeds run much longer than speed 6. The gauge in the handle shows the charge left, and the included cable recharges it.

What is in the box?

The Standard package contains the blower, a charging cable and the user manual. The Complete kit adds the air nozzle for inflating, a dust collection cup, a suction hose and a spare filter.

Will I pay customs or import fees?

Orders to Germany, Sweden, Denmark and Finland include all VAT. For the UK and Norway, import VAT is collected at checkout, so there is nothing to pay on delivery. Iceland is the only destination where local import VAT may be charged on arrival.

Can I take it on a plane?

It runs on a lithium-ion battery, so it belongs in your hand luggage. Check your airline's rules before you fly.

How do returns work?

Email us within 30 days of delivery and we send you a return label. You get your refund within 14 days of us receiving the parcel. Your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal in the EU and UK applies in full.
