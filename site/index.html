Questions

Before you buy.

Will it arrive before Christmas?

Yes, if you order by the date shown for your country in the delivery table. After that date we still ship the same day, but we can't promise delivery before 24 December.

Can I send it straight to the person it's for?

Yes. Enter their address at checkout and tick gift wrapping. The parcel has no price inside and your message goes on the card.

Is it useful at a track day?

Yes. It fits in a kit bag or the boot, runs on its own battery and needs no compressor or power socket. Use it on the high speeds for brake ducts, wheel arches and radiators, and on vacuum for the footwells.

Does it work as a vacuum?

Yes. Fit the dust collection cup, filter and suction hose from the Complete kit, and it pulls crumbs, sand and dust into the cup. It suits car interiors, desks and keyboards. It is not a replacement for a full-size vacuum cleaner.

Is it safe for electronics and a sim rig?

Yes. It uses no propellant, so there is no cold spray and no residue. Use speed 1 or 2 for keyboards, wheel bases and circuit boards, and hold fan blades still while you clean them.

What is it made of?

The housing is ABS plastic, a tough, impact-resistant material widely used for power tools and electronics.

Will I pay customs or import fees?

Orders to Germany, Sweden, Denmark and Finland include all VAT. For the UK and Norway, import VAT is collected at checkout, so there is nothing to pay on delivery. Iceland is the only destination where local import VAT may be charged on arrival.

What if it's the wrong gift?

Orders placed from 1 November can be returned until 31 January. Email us and we send you a return label. Your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal in the EU and UK applies in full.
